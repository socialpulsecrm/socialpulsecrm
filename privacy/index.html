1. Information We Collect

We collect the following types of information:

  • Account information: name, email address, username and password when you register.
  • Connected platform data: OAuth access tokens, profile information, posts, analytics and engagement metrics from social media accounts you choose to connect (including Facebook Pages, Instagram, YouTube, TikTok, X/Twitter, LinkedIn, Threads, Bluesky, Facebook Pages, Facebook Groups, Pinterest, Reddit, Mastodon, and Google Business).
  • Content you create: scheduled posts, captions, media, campaign briefs and any other content you draft or publish using the Service.
  • Usage data: how you interact with the Service — pages visited, features used, clicks, and session duration — collected via server logs and first-party analytics.
  • Device information: browser type, operating system, IP address and general geographic region.
  • Billing information: payment method details processed securely by our third-party payment processor (Stripe). We do not store full card numbers on our servers.

2. How We Use Your Information

  • To provide, maintain and improve the Service
  • To authenticate you and keep your account secure
  • To publish content to your connected social media accounts when you initiate it
  • To display analytics and performance data from your own accounts
  • To power AI-assisted features such as caption drafting, reply suggestions and trend insights
  • To process subscription payments and manage your billing
  • To send service-related notifications, product updates and support responses
  • To ensure security, detect fraud and prevent abuse
  • To comply with our legal obligations

We do not sell, rent or share your data with third parties for advertising.

3. Data Sharing

We do not sell your personal information. We may share data with:

  • Third-party platforms: only as necessary to perform actions you initiate (for example, publishing a post to Facebook, Instagram or LinkedIn via their official APIs).
  • Meta (Facebook): when you connect a Facebook Page or Instagram account, data is exchanged with Meta's Graph API under Meta's Platform Terms. If you remove Social Pulse from your Facebook Apps & Websites settings, Meta notifies us and we delete your associated data. See our Facebook Data Deletion Instructions.
  • Payment processor: Stripe processes billing information on our behalf and is PCI-DSS compliant.
  • Infrastructure & analytics providers: hosting, database and first-party analytics providers who process data solely on our behalf under data processing agreements.
  • Legal requirements: when required by applicable law, court order, or to protect the rights and safety of Social Pulse or our users.
  • Business transfers: in the event of a merger, acquisition or sale of all or a portion of our assets, user data may be transferred as part of that transaction.

4. Data Storage & Security

We store data on secure servers with encryption in transit (TLS 1.2+) and at rest (AES-256). OAuth tokens for connected accounts are encrypted using AES-256-GCM before storage, and data is held in secured PostgreSQL databases with regular automated backups. We follow industry-standard security practices including two-factor authentication for internal systems, role-based access controls, and audit logging. We conduct periodic security reviews and promptly address reported vulnerabilities.

5. Data Retention

We retain your data for as long as your account is active or as needed to provide the Service. Upon account deletion, we remove your personal data within 30 days, except where retention is required by law (for example, billing records which may be kept for up to 7 years for tax purposes). Disconnecting a social media account revokes our access token; any cached data from that platform is deleted within 30 days.

6. Your Rights

Depending on your jurisdiction (including under India's DPDP Act, GDPR, CCPA and international privacy frameworks), you have the right to:

  • Access your personal data we hold
  • Correct inaccurate or incomplete data
  • Delete your account and all associated data
  • Port your data in a machine-readable format
  • Object to or restrict certain processing activities
  • Withdraw consent at any time where processing is based on consent
  • Right to Nominate: nominate any individual to manage or access your data in the event of death or incapacity (under Section 14 of the DPDP Act)
  • Right of Grievance Redressal: directly register concerns with our designated Grievance Officer
  • Disconnect any linked social media account at any time
  • Facebook data deletion: request removal of Facebook-sourced data by following our Facebook Data Deletion Instructions

To exercise any of these rights, contact us at privacy@socialpulsecrm.com or our Grievance Officer at grievance@socialpulsecrm.com. We will respond within 30 days (or within 7 business days for statutory DPDP grievances).

7. Digital Personal Data Protection Act, 2023 (India Compliance)

Social Pulse operates in full compliance with the Digital Personal Data Protection (DPDP) Act, 2023 of India:

  • Grounds for Processing: We process digital personal data based on freely given, specific, informed, unconditional, and unambiguous consent, or for specified legitimate uses such as providing contracted CRM features, processing payments, and statutory recordkeeping.
  • Data Minimization & Purpose Limitation: We collect only the data necessary to provide multi-platform publishing, analytics, and CRM services. We never retain personal data longer than necessary for the stated purpose.
  • Cross-Border Data Transfers: Personal data is processed on secure cloud infrastructure adhering to strict security controls (AES-256 encryption at rest, TLS 1.3 in transit). We do not transfer personal data to any country or territory restricted by the Central Government of India under Section 16 of the DPDP Act.
  • Protection of Minors: The Service is not intended for individuals under 18 years of age. We do not knowingly process personal data of children, undertake behavioral tracking, or direct targeted advertisements toward minors.
  • Right to Nominate (Section 14): You may nominate an individual who shall exercise your rights under the DPDP Act in the event of death or incapacity. To submit a nomination, email grievance@socialpulsecrm.com with your account details.
  • Statutory Grievance Redressal Officer: In compliance with Section 13 of the DPDP Act and the Information Technology Rules, we have established an expedited grievance mechanism:

    Designation: Data Protection & Grievance Redressal Officer

    Dedicated Email: grievance@socialpulsecrm.com

    Alternate Contact: privacy@socialpulsecrm.com

    Resolution Timelines: Acknowledged within 24 hours; resolved within 7 business days. If a grievance remains unresolved, you have the right to lodge a complaint with the Data Protection Board of India.

8. Cookies & Tracking Technologies

We use essential cookies for authentication and secure session management. We may use privacy-respecting first-party analytics cookies to understand aggregate usage patterns without tracking you across third-party websites. We do not sell user data or deploy third-party advertising tracking cookies. For a detailed breakdown of the cookies we use and how to customize your preferences, please review our dedicated Cookie Policy.

9. Third-Party Links & Children's Privacy

The Service may contain links to third-party websites; we are not responsible for their privacy practices. The Service is not directed at children under the age of 18, and we do not knowingly collect personal data from minors. If you believe we have inadvertently collected such data, please contact us immediately so we can promptly delete it.

10. Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. When we make material changes, we will update the "Last updated" date at the top of this page and notify you via email or in-app notification at least 14 days before changes take effect. Continued use of the Service after the effective date constitutes your acceptance of the updated policy.

11. Contact & Grievance Redressal

For privacy inquiries, data subject access requests, or regulatory questions, contact our privacy team at privacy@socialpulsecrm.com or our Grievance Redressal Officer at grievance@socialpulsecrm.com. You may also write to us at the address provided on our Contact page. If you are located in the European Economic Area or India and have an unresolved dispute, you have the right to lodge a complaint with your competent supervisory authority or the Data Protection Board of India.
