1. What Are Cookies?

Cookies are small text files that are stored on your device (computer, smartphone, or tablet) when you visit a website. They allow websites to remember your device, preferences, and secure login state across browser visits so you do not have to re-authenticate or reconfigure your settings repeatedly.

2. How Social Pulse Uses Cookies

Social Pulse believes in strict data minimization and user privacy. We do not deploy third-party cross-site advertising cookies or sell user data. We use cookies strictly to:

  • Authenticate users and maintain secure active sessions
  • Prevent Cross-Site Request Forgery (CSRF) and replay attacks
  • Remember UI preferences such as theme (dark/light mode) and active workspace
  • Gather privacy-respecting, aggregated performance metrics to ensure server health and fast load times

3. Categories of Cookies We Use

We classify our cookies into the following functional categories:

  • Strictly Necessary Cookies: These cookies are essential for the operation of our platform. They enable secure authentication, multi-tenant isolation, CSRF protection, and account access. You cannot opt out of these cookies because the service cannot function without them.
  • Functional & Preference Cookies: These cookies enable the site to remember choices you make (such as active tenant, sidebar collapse state, and timezone settings) to provide a tailored, seamless experience.
  • Aggregated Analytics Cookies: We use privacy-preserving, first-party analytics to measure site traffic and feature adoption without tracking your personal identity or browsing behavior across external websites.

5. Local Storage & Session Storage

In addition to HTTP cookies, our web application utilizes browser HTML5 Local Storage and Session Storage to store client-side state (such as composer drafts in progress, cached navigation menus, and client theme settings). This data remains exclusively in your local browser sandbox and is never transmitted to third parties.

6. Managing Your Cookie Preferences

You can adjust your cookie settings at any time:

  • Website Banner: You can click "Accept All" or "Strictly Necessary Only" when prompted by our cookie banner on our marketing site.
  • Browser Controls: Most modern browsers (Chrome, Safari, Firefox, Edge) allow you to refuse, delete, or block cookies through browser preferences. Note that blocking essential cookies will break login sessions and prevent you from accessing your Social Pulse dashboard.

7. Questions & Contact

If you have any questions about our use of cookies or privacy practices, please contact our team at privacy@socialpulsecrm.com or review our Privacy Policy and Terms of Service.
